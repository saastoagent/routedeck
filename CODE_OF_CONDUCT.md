# Code of Conduct

RouteDeck contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free regardless of background,
experience, identity, or viewpoint.

Constructive technical disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment,
threats, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, and publication of
another person's private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository's GitHub private vulnerability-reporting form and label the report
as a conduct concern. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical;
the project does not yet promise a response SLA.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly
representing RouteDeck.
